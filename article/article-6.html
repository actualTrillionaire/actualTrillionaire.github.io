DSA and their Stance on Prison Abolition

Primary Sources:
https://program.dsausa.org/
https://www.dsausa.org/working-groups/abolition-working-group/

The Official Stance, Published on DSA’s Website

END MASS INCARCERATION AND POLICE IMMUNITY
Demilitarize police departments, disempower police unions, and redirect funding to public services as steps towards fully abolishing the police and prison system which protects the rich and jails the poor. Treat drug addiction and mental illness as health issues, not crimes. End qualified immunity and empower civilian oversight bodies with real authority over the police.

We are committed to the horizon of abolition and the path leading us there. Our demands:

  • Defund the police by rejecting any expansion to police budgets or scope of enforcement while cutting budgets annually towards zero
  • End the criminalization of working-class survival
  • Freedom for all incarcerated people
  • Demilitarize the police and end colonial policing of our cities and neighborhoods
  • Freedom of working-class self-organization and democratic political action
  • Invest in community self-governance and care, not cops

We’ll take a quick look at some major politicians supporting or opposing these policies: Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, Zohran Mamdani, and the DSA winners in the most recent midterms.

AOC and Mamdani

AOC once expressed support for defunding police and prisons during the pandemic-era climate. Reiterating her stances in August 2026, she’s distanced herself from that rhetoric.

“I have a local city councilman that has this saying: ‘Woke 1 (Pandemic-era discourse) was crazy’… especially during Covid, I think that the doors were really open in trying to entertain any and every policy that was going to get us to a better place. And I actually think that the discussions that were had in that time were quite fruitful. I think that when we talk about crime today, it’s fundamentally different than the way that we talked about crime in bringing crime down. And I think that we all share in the goals of having as low a crime rate as possible. I think that, you know, during lockdown, of course, rhetoric in that time is not rhetoric that we would use today. And I’m grateful that these candidates have the opportunity to present who they are to their electorate and what they’re campaigning on in this moment.”

Zohran Mamdani around this time during the pandemic took a hardline stance against police department and prison funding, with notable claims shown below (via X):

“We don't need an investigation to know that the NYPD is racist, anti-queer & a major threat to public safety. What we need is to #DefundTheNYPD. But your deal with @NYCMayor uses budget tricks to keep as many cops as possible on the beat. NO to fake cuts - defund the police.”

Around this time in 2020, when asked to clarify points about prison reform, Mamdani said:

“What purpose do they serve? I think a lot of people who defend the carceral state, they defend the idea of it and the way it makes them feel. They’re not defending the reality of it and the practices that are part and parcel of it. If you actually break it down and ask people how many people come out of the prison system better than they went into the prison system, how much harm is actually being prevented versus created?”

Mamdani’s recent interview in November of 2026 with Fox News’s Shannon Bream voiced explicit pushback against abolition of prisons, abolition of borders, the abolition of the Supreme Court, and abolishing the Senate. When asked about this dissent from the DSA’s official stances (Shannon says “It sounds like you’re not aligned with some of the core things they really want to get done”), Mamdani responds:

“No. You know, when you go into a room and there are more than one socialist in the room, you will often find disagreement in that room, and what unites members of the Democratic Socialists of America is, in many ways, what has brought people into politics over the years –– a belief in the dignity of working-class people and the dignity that has to be a characteristic of their lives.”

https://thehill.com/homenews/state-watch/6206862-mamdani-ice-abolition-statement/

The 6 DSA Midterm Winners on Prison Abolition

Here’s what the 6 big DSA midterm winners have to say about Prison Abolition (6 Democratic Socialists of America candidates elected to House):

  • Melat Kiros, Colorado - no official stance (Campaign Site)
  • Angela Gonzales-Torres, California - campaigns on reform, not abolition (Platform)
  • Donovan McKinney, Michigan - campaigns on reform, not abolition (Positions)
  • Chris Rabb, Pennsylvania - campaigns on reform, not abolition (Platform)
  • Claire Valdez, New York - no official stance (Issues)
  • Darializa Avila Chevalier, New York - campaigns on abolition (Yahoo News)

In an interview with Vanity Fair on October 30th, 2026, right before the midterms, Darializa Avila Chevalier doesn’t commit to a straight answer whether a murderer should be locked away in prison. This comes after Chevalier called for the arrest of an ICE agent who murdered a migrant, 28, in the Bronx earlier that month.

"I'm just curious about now, now that you're going to become a lawmaker, do you believe that someone who is convicted of murder with intent should be physically confined to protect the public?" the reporter asked.
"But that is what happens in the day-to-day … What I argue as someone who deeply cares about ending violence in our communities is that if we don't begin the process now of building this world where we are proactive in preventing violence instead of reactive, once the violence has already happened, then we're never going to get to that world."

Pandemic-Era Bonus Story: Rashida Tlaib

Rashida Tlaib, US Representative to Michigan since 2019, took a controversial stance towards prison abolition in 2021. Tlaib endorsed support for the BREATHE Act which proposed to empty federal prisons (12% of prisons nationwide) within a decade, with the goal of being half-empty over the span of 5 years. Axios correspondent Jonathan Swan presses Rep. Tlaib in the below video where Rep. Tlaib reaffirms her stance and defends it.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GwWKmOSlEhg

Tlaib is the first Palestinian American, and one of the first two Muslims, to be elected to Congress, fueling fire to the controversy.
